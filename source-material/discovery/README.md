# Discovery Evidence — Expert-Buyer Side

This folder is Kodara's first body of **customer-contact evidence**. Until now the CPO agent reasoned from product books (the `source-material/*-summary.md` files) and the VSL transcript. Those describe *theory* and *promised value*. This folder describes what real prospects actually said.

## What's here

- **`onboarding-jtbd-2026-06-synthesis.md`** — the cross-call rollup. Start here. Portfolio-level patterns across all 15 calls, every claim annotated with how many of the 15 clients support it.
- **`calls/`** — the 15 individual per-call JTBD analyses, one per onboarding call (May 5 – June 9, 2026).
- **`onboarding-jtbd-analysis-prompt.md`** — the prompt used to produce each per-call analysis (provenance).
- **`onboarding-jtbd-synthesis-prompt.md`** — the prompt used to produce the synthesis (provenance).
- **`expert-side-opportunity-solution-tree.md`** — the OST derived from the synthesis; the structured reasoning scaffold for roadmap decisions on the expert side.

## Provenance

Source = 15 Kodara onboarding-call transcripts (Fathom), analyzed independently through a Jobs to be Done lens, then synthesized. These are the **founder-led Step-1 onboarding calls** Lucas runs personally.

## How to weight this evidence (read before citing)

These are **onboarding calls**, which means:

1. **Expert-buyer side only.** This tells you about the person who *pays* (the expert/business owner), not the end-user/lead who actually uses the product daily. The consumer-side tree is still unbuilt — these calls do not fill it.
2. **Stated intent at the point of sale.** Buyers describe what they're *picturing*. Treat this as **desirability evidence** (what they want, what pushes/pulls/scares them), not as proof of *experienced* value or post-launch behavior.
3. **Partly hypothetical.** Where a quote is about a specific past behavior ("the last time I…"), it's strong story-based signal. Where it's about an imagined future ("I want it to…"), apply the say-vs-do discount.
4. **A snapshot, not continuous discovery.** This is the June 2026 batch. It goes stale. The intent is a recurring monthly pipeline (fathom pull → per-call JTBD → synthesis → `discovery/YYYY-MM.md`) so the agent can diff month-over-month.

Net: cite this confidently for **expert-side push/pull/anxiety/jobs and desirability**. Do **not** cite it for end-user behavior, ascension, drop-off, or churn — those remain unverified and must come from consumer-side contact and instrumentation.
