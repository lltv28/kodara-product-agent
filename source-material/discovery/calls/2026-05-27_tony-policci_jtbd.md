## 1. The Switch Event

**What was happening right before he reached out.** Tony is a 30-plus-year veteran direct-response copywriter and marketing consultant who has fallen out of the position he once held at the top of his field. He describes his current state as treading water: *"what I'm doing now, what I've been doing still to just, you know, keep the lights on is I'm writing copy for people. But I'm tired of doing that."* He is doing project-management and creative-strategy work for a coach (Shanda Sumter) and a few of her clients, but *"I'm still trading hours for dollars, right? I don't have equity. I'm not creating royalty... but I'm time bankrupt."*

**The breaking point / moment of frustration.** Two compounding pressures. First, financial: *"none of which are bringing in the revenue that I need right now at this phase in my life,"* and the sting of going backward after being at the top, *"I never was at a point where I was going negative in my business for a long period of time... that's hard as hell... especially for when you've been at the top of your game... that's hard on the male psyche."* Second, identity/mortality at 63: *"that's not what I want my kids to be talking about... when I'm gone. I'm like, yeah, my dad was such a great copywriter... I literally don't care about being a copywriter."* He wants to be remembered as a warrior, a man of God, someone who changed lives.

**The trigger to act.** He saw a Kodara ad: *"all of that leads to, you know, I see your ad, I saw what you guys were doing, and I was telling Nora that, you know, you've got an irresistible offer."* The intro call with Noor framed an "AI version of Tony," which crystallized the appeal: *"that's really what... I need at this point is a new version of me that runs 24 seven... while I sleep, that's producing sales and I'm only getting in front of people... when we're talking high ticket."*

**What he had tried before and why it fell short.**
- **Equity/royalty bets via other people's businesses** (Dean Graziosi, 2007-2014): *"that, like, was supposed to be a great equity position for me. It turned out to not be. A lot of promises were made."* He blames over-trusting and weak contracting: *"I should have, you know, ended up with eight figures and, you know, barely wound up with six."*
- **His own agency, Copy Avengers (2018):** clients defaulted, *"literally every single one of them defaulted... to the tune of a hundred thousand dollars... I paid my copywriters to do the work and I didn't collect."*
- **Two AI Summits (first ~July 2024):** *"Those were not successful, in my judgment,"* because speakers (even his brother Joe Polish with a near-million list) didn't email, *"they just did social media promotion,"* so the engaged list and high-ticket sales never materialized: *"none of which bought at the... live web class."*
- **A neglected 7,000-person list** that went *"dead cold"* during the Dean years (revived only ~200).
- **A nearly finished restaurant course** (Connection Multiplier, 10 modules) that is built but not yet monetized.

## 3. Functional, Emotional, and Social Jobs

**Functional job.** Convert decades of marketing/copywriting expertise plus personal-transformation knowledge into a productized, AI-powered asset that earns money without consuming his hours: *"How can I take my intellectual capital and leverage it to something that somebody actually wants?"* and *"a new version of me that runs 24 seven... that's producing sales."* The immediate sub-job is cash flow to fund what he cares about: *"I want to create something that's going to generate revenue for me so that I can fund these other things that I'm passionate about."*

**Emotional job.** To feel relevant, in control, and at peace again after going backward. He wants relief from the time-bankruptcy and the shame of negative cash flow (*"that's hard on the male psyche... I've got to manage my mind an awful lot"*), and he wants to feel excited and personally aligned: *"I really would love to... make something that aligns with what I care about and think is important."*

**Social job.** To be remembered, by his sons and his world, as a man of substance rather than a marketer-for-hire: *"I want him to say... my dad was, he was like a warrior, man... he loved people. He changed lives. He helped guys... believe in themselves and transform... And he was a man of God."* He also wants a brand identity that finally fits ("Tony Stark"), versus the ill-fitting *"Iron Man of copywriting"* label: *"that doesn't really feel like it."*

**Cognitive dissonance flag.** His **stated functional ask** is revenue-first (*"what's the immediate want? I want to create something that's going to generate revenue"*), but his **underlying driver** is legacy and meaning (*"I literally don't care about being a copywriter"* / kids remembering him as a warrior and man of God). He rationalizes the meaningful work as instrumental ("fund these other things") yet it is clearly the real prize. Second tension: he holds elite copywriting credibility (*"$400 million in sales," "top 100 copywriting books"*) but actively wants to walk away from it, which is the exact asset Kodara would normally monetize fastest. Naming and resolving this gap is the central strategic decision of the engagement.

## 6. Stall Risk Score

**Score: 4 / 5 (elevated stall risk).**

Justification: The capability and inputs are strong (he is responsive, committed to sending content by end of next day, and highly trusting of Lucas), but the engagement has no settled offer or niche and the client is a self-described chronic overthinker with a lifelong pattern of fragmented focus and projects that never lasted, plus active pivot fear ("are those things going to land?"). Those decision-and-follow-through risks, not delivery capacity, are what most threaten a fast launch.

Recommended intervention: On the promised follow-up call, do not present options. Make one decisive recommendation (the blue-ocean men's transformation/accountability brand he lit up about), lock it in writing as v1.0 direction, and immediately hand him a single, narrow, deadline-bound homework (the raw founder-story recording) so analysis paralysis has nothing to chew on and momentum is forced.
