● Locked · Surface 01

The sharpie live-drawing

A visual primitive draws itself stroke by stroke in a hand-sketched style, beside voice mode. The magic is the drawing, not the drawn. Buildable via stroke-dashoffset animation + roughjs on top of the template system.

Let me show you. Most of your effort is stuck down here — but your AI product lives up here.

Vocabulary — the universal six: two-by-two (tradeoff), line-goes-up (trajectory), this-vs-that (contrast), the ladder (progression), before/after (transformation), process arrow (sequence). A second tier of domain templates sits below, selected per avatar. Because the shapes are known, every stroke path is known — which is exactly what makes the live-drawing cheap. The dumb-simple decision and the holy-shit decision are the same decision.
● Locked · Surfaces 02 + 03

Cited proof & the mixed-source row

Social proof disguised as a citation. Alone, proof reads as marketing; sitting in a sources row beside a neutral knowledge citation, it reads as evidence. The highest-value proof stays expanded by default so the result is seen without any action.

Put a small paid offer in front of your audience before spending on new ads — a buyer who's paid you anything is already invested and acts on your advice.1

This advice helped Leanne make back over 100% of her ad spend on the front end, then enroll $19,000 in clients from one month of ads.

the highest-value proof stays open by default

Restraint is the feature. A footnote appears only on the two or three most load-bearing, most-doubted claims — the engine knows which. Citing the expert's own brain does double duty: it answers the field's #1 anxiety ("is this really me, or is it making things up?") by proving fidelity in real time.
● Locked · Surface 05

The two-futures fork

Future-pacing and honest scarcity are one lever pointed two ways. The diverging lines create the felt gap; the cards below fill in the specifics. Feeling opens them, specifics close them — in that order.

I keep telling myself I'll deal with this next quarter. Is that fine?

Here's the honest fork. Same starting point, two very different years.

↗ if you build this
✓ marketing pays for itself by month 2
✓ 12+ high-ticket clients this year
✓ the business runs without you
→ if nothing changes
– still trading hours for every sale
– 99% of leads keep walking away

Deployed live: the two lines draw themselves as the AI narrates the fork, then the cards populate one row at a time — every bullet built from what the buyer told the AI earlier. Both paths must come from the buyer's real inputs: the green path stays inside what the product can deliver, the gray path is their genuine status quo, never doom-tuned.
